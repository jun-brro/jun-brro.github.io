Bio

Hello 👋 I'm incoming MS student at ALIN Lab (KAIST), starting Fall 2026. I will be advised by prof. Jinwoo Shin.

I believe that the fusion of various modalities, particularly centered around language models, is essential for AI to positively impact society. Especically I'm interested in VLA models that can effectively understand and learn from action label free datasets. Also, I am discovering the effective representation learning of generative models.
