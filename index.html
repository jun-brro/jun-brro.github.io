About

I'm an incoming MS student at ALIN Lab (KAIST), starting Fall 2026, advised by Prof. Jinwoo Shin.

I believe the fusion of modalities—centered around language models—is essential for AI to positively impact society. I'm especially interested in VLA models that can understand and learn from action-label-free datasets, and in the effective representation learning of generative models.
